# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: local and small-business owners in southeast Wisconsin (builders, plumbers, trade services, restaurants/food, small B2B ecommerce) deciding whether to hire Cody to design, build, or grow their website. They are evaluating on trust and proof of results, usually on a phone, often referred.

Secondary: employers and recruiters evaluating Cody for marketing/web roles. Served by the résumé page as a supporting credential — the site does not favor this audience.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio + client-acquisition site for Cody Butryn (codybutryn.com). Success = a visitor contacts Cody to start a project (email, call, or LinkedIn). The résumé page's success = printed/saved as PDF.

## Positioning

"A web designer with a marketer's scoreboard." Cody is not a pure designer: 8+ years across business development, content marketing, and sales, plus a founder who scaled his own company (Miltown Eats) from zero with his own money on the line. Every project is presented with its business result — revenue, leads, orders — not just visuals. A competing freelancer showing only screenshots cannot truthfully copy this claim.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from referrals, LinkedIn, or local search. Trade-business owners evaluate quickly and on mobile; tappable phone/email matters. The résumé page doubles as the actual print résumé (window.print → PDF, print styles are load-bearing).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static hand-coded HTML/CSS/JS, three pages (index, gallery, resume) + `assets/`. No framework, no build step — and "designed & built by hand — this site is the portfolio" is a live claim in the footer, so the shipped code quality is itself evidence.
- Gallery has filter buttons (all/web/branding/ads/video) and placeholder "slot" cards awaiting real work samples. Placeholders are honest ("Sample — replace") — do not fabricate work to fill them.
- Site title currently prefixed "Mockup —": the incumbent design is itself a draft, not a shipped brand.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Cody Butryn; wordmark "CB". Domain codybutryn.com.
- Voice: direct, numbers-first, no-nonsense; trades-adjacent plain speech ("spec'd like a job", "straight answers, fixed scope").
- No other visual commitments — the incumbent blueprint/drafting aesthetic (Archivo/IBM Plex Mono, orange #F4581C, grid backgrounds) is a draft direction, replaceable by redesign.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over polish: every project shown must carry its business result; work without numbers doesn't make the page.
2. The site is the portfolio: shipped code quality, speed, and accessibility are themselves the demo.
3. Serve the phone-holding trade owner first: tappable contact, fast load, instant clarity about what Cody does.
4. Honest evidence only: no invented testimonials, metrics, or filler work samples.
5. Freelance conversion first; résumé is a supporting credential, not a co-equal goal.
